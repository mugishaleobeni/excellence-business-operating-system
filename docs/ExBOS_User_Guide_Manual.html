01 Platform Overview & Getting Started

Excellence Business Operating System (ExBOS) is an all-in-one cloud business operating system engineered for Rwandan and East African enterprises. Unlike basic sales apps that mix revenue, credit, and cash, ExBOS connects inventory, purchasing, sales, received payments, customer debts, supplier payables, operating expenses, and cash flow into one strictly isolated, audited workspace per business.

1.1 Creating Your Account (7-Step Guided Registration)

  1. Step 1 — Account Credentials: Enter your full name, business email address, WhatsApp-ready phone number, and a strong password (protected with Argon2id hashing).
  2. Step 2 — Email Verification: ExBOS sends a branded email containing a 6-digit verification code plus two 1-click buttons: Verify Email Instantly and Copy Code & Auto-Fill. Clicking the verified link automatically confirms your email across tabs and devices.
  3. Step 3 — Choose Your Business Architecture: Select the exact operating model that fits your business (Make & Sell, Buy & Sell, Services, Agriculture & Livestock, Food & Hospitality, or Hybrid).
  4. Step 4 — Business Profile: Enter your official Business Name, category, country, district/location, and default currency (RWF).
  5. Step 5 — Configure Workspace: Confirm your primary operational modules and optional language preference (English or Kinyarwanda).
  6. Step 6 — Choose Subscription Plan: Start on the Free plan or select a paid tier (Bronze, Silver, or Gold). For paid plans, ExBOS displays the official MTN MoMo / Airtel Money payment number (0781 963 981 / +250 781 963 981) and asks you to paste your payment confirmation SMS message copy or upload a screenshot proof to record your payment timestamp and activate your 30-day cycle.
  7. Step 7 — Complete Setup: Your isolated zero-base workspace initializes at 0 RWF with no fake or shared records, ready for your real entries.

1.2 Signing In, 2-Step Verification & Password Recovery

  • Standard Login: Sign in at /login with your registered email and password. Sessions use encrypted JWT tokens synchronized with MongoDB Atlas.
  • Optional 2-Step Email Verification (2FA): Enable 2-Step Verification in Settings → Business Profile to require a 6-digit email OTP (with 1-click auto-fill link) on every login.
  • Forgot Password: Click Forgot Password? on the login page to receive a 6-digit reset code and verified reset link valid for 15 minutes.

02 Installing the Offline PWA App & Language Switching

2.1 Installable Progressive Web App (Phone & PC)

ExBOS is a full Progressive Web App (PWA). Installing it gives you a native app icon on your phone home screen or computer desktop, faster startup, and automatic offline resilience:

  • Android / Chrome / Edge: Tap the Install ExBOS App banner at the bottom of the screen (or browser menu → Install App).
  • iPhone / iPad (Safari): Tap the Share icon in Safari and select Add to Home Screen.
  • Live Phone ↔ PC Cloud Sync: Products, sales, and purchases entered on your phone automatically synchronize to your PC dashboard in real time whenever both devices are online.
  • Offline Resilience: If internet drops temporarily, entries are queued locally and synced automatically once connectivity returns.

2.2 Instant Language Switching (English & Kinyarwanda)

Use the Language Dropdown in the top header or Settings to switch instantaneously between English and Kinyarwanda. Currency amounts are also converted into readable words in your selected language.

05 Products, Inventory & Camera Barcode Scanner

  • Adding Products: Go to Products → All Products (or tap the New Product Quick Link) to record product name, category, SKU/barcode, unit of measure, opening stock, unit purchase cost, selling price, low-stock threshold, and optional expiry date.
  • Camera Barcode & QR Scanner: Click the camera scan icon inside the Product or Sales modal to scan any product barcode or QR code directly with your phone or laptop camera—automatically looking up existing items or filling the SKU field.

06 Sales, POS Checkout & Customer Credit

  • Recording a Sale: Select a product from your catalog (unit price and available stock load automatically), enter quantity, choose customer (walk-in or named credit customer), and select the payment channel (Cash, MTN MoMo Business, Airtel Money, Bank Transfer, or Customer Credit).
  • Payment Status Separation:
    • Paid in Full: Full sale amount increases both Sales Revenue and Actual Payments Received in your chosen cash/MoMo/bank channel.
    • Part-Paid: Only the cash paid today enters your liquid balance; the remaining unpaid portion is automatically added to Customers → Outstanding Balances.
    • Unpaid (Customer Credit): Increases Sales Revenue and Customer Receivables, but adds 0 RWF to spendable cash until the customer actually pays.
  • Collecting Customer Debt: Open Customers → Outstanding Balances or Reports and click Record Payment to collect full or partial debt repayments.

07 Intelligent Purchasing & Financial Awareness Engine

ExBOS transforms purchasing from a passive form into a live financial advisor. Every time you open New Purchase (in Buy & Sell, Make & Sell, or any architecture), the engine evaluates your inventory, sales velocity, open purchase orders, supplier price history, and real cash affordability in real time.

Available Purchasing Budget = Available Business Cash (Cash + MoMo + Bank)
                                  − Reserved Operational Funds (default 15% of OpEx + unpaid bills)
                                  − Existing Open Purchase Order Commitments
Purchase Shortfall = Immediate Cash Outlay − Available Purchasing Budget

7.1 Live Assessment & Smart Recommendations

  • Stock & Demand Awareness: Alerts you when stock is 0 or below reorder level, estimates how many days of expected sales your current stock covers, and warns you if you already have units on an Open Purchase Order to prevent duplicate ordering.
  • Lower Supplier Price Alert: Compares your current unit cost against historical purchases and notifies you if another supplier previously offered a lower price.
  • 1-Click Shortfall Resolution Options: Whenever a purchase exceeds your available purchasing budget, ExBOS clearly displays the shortfall and provides four 1-click resolutions:
    1. I Have Other Side Money I Can Add (+X RWF): Lets the business owner add personal/external side funds to afford the purchase. ExBOS records this as an audited Owner Side Money / Capital Top-Up inflow so your cash ledger stays 100% reconciled.
    2. Reduce Quantity to Affordable Units: Automatically adjusts the order quantity to the maximum units your current budget can cover.
    3. Pay Available Budget Now & Put Remainder on Supplier Credit: Splits the invoice into Part-Paid so cash never goes negative while tracking supplier debt accurately.
    4. Save as Open Purchase Order (Pay on Delivery): Records the commitment without immediate cash outflow and without adding stock until goods physically arrive.

7.2 Purchase Orders vs. Received Inventory & Partial Payments

  • Received Immediately: Adds full quantity to shelf stock immediately.
  • Purchase Order / In Transit: Tracks the order as an open commitment (receivedQuantity = 0). Stock increases only when you update the PO to Partially Received or Received.
  • No Double Deductions: Paying a supplier balance later via Pay Supplier updates the purchase's paid total and supplier payable balance without double-counting the original invoice cost.

08 Where Is My Money? (8-Bucket Reconciliation)

Located under Finance / Where Is My Money, this executive panel answers the single most important question for every business owner: Where is my money sitting right now, and how much can I safely spend?

Financial Bucket / Metric What It Measures & Why It Matters
Cash in Hand (Till / Drawer) Physical cash reconciled from cash sales, collections, and cash expenses/purchases.
Mobile Money (MTN MoMo / Airtel) Liquid business mobile wallet balances separated from personal spending.
Bank Accounts (BK / Equity / BPR) Reconciled bank transfer inflows and supplier/operating bank outflows.
Stock & Materials (Non-Spendable) Inventory sitting on shelves valued at purchase cost—shown beside cash, never added to spendable cash.
Owed to Me (Customer Debts) Unpaid customer invoices tracked separately—never treated as spendable cash until collected.
Money I Owe (Supplier Payables) Unpaid balances owed to vendors on received goods.
Gross Profit & Net Profit Gross Profit = Sales Revenue − COGS; Net Profit = Gross Profit − Operating Expenses.
Available Purchasing Budget Liquid cash minus operational safety reserves and open Purchase Order commitments.

09 Optional RRA Tax-Ready Compliance & Export Pack

ExBOS includes an optional RRA Tax-Ready Pack designed specifically for Rwanda Revenue Authority (RRA) preparation. Businesses that are not yet ready for RRA reporting can leave it turned off—it is completely opt-in.

  • Opt-In / Opt-Out Master Switch: Navigate to Settings → Tax & RRA Profile and toggle Enable RRA Tax-Ready Features on or off at any time.
  • Tax Profile Configuration: Enter your 9-digit RRA TIN, tax regime (Flat / Micro, 3% Quarterly Turnover Tax, or Real Regime — 18% VAT & CIT/PIT), VAT registration status, and EBM readiness.
  • RRA Tax Center (Reports → RRA Tax Center): View real-time Output VAT (18%), Claimable Input VAT, Net VAT Payable/Credit, Estimated 3% Turnover Tax, and Estimated Net Profit for any date range or branch.
  • 1-Click CSV Exports & Accountant Email: Download itemized RRA Sales Annex CSV, RRA Purchases Annex CSV, or Expenses Ledger CSV, or click Send Pack to Accountant to dispatch a branded ExBOS summary email directly to your accountant.

11 Multi-Branch Control & Team Permissions

11.1 Creating & Managing Branches (Main HQ)

Business owners on eligible plans can create isolated sub-branches in Settings → Branches & Multi-Location Control:

  • Branch Isolation: Each branch maintains its own sales, purchases, expenses, and stock records while Main HQ views consolidated performance in the Main HQ · Branch Activity & Best Performer dashboard card (Contribution Heatmap + Best Performing Branch Ranking).
  • Branch Manager Invitations: Assign a dedicated Branch Manager with their own login credentials. They receive a branded ExBOS email invitation and are automatically locked to their assigned branch.
  • HQ Security Controls: Configure per-branch permissions (Can Delete Records, Can Edit Prices, Can View Profit Reports, Can Record Expenses, and Maximum Expense Limit in RWF), require an Owner PIN to switch back to Main HQ, or Freeze a branch (read-only mode) instantly.
  • HQ-to-Branch Stock Transfers: Transfer inventory quantities from Main HQ directly into any sub-branch with automatic stock deduction at HQ and restock at the destination branch.

11.2 Team Members & Role-Based Access

Under Settings → Team & Permissions, invite staff members (Cashier, Store Manager, Accountant, or Branch Manager) with granular module permissions. Staff members automatically inherit the owner's subscription tier.

14.2 End-to-End Data Relationship Flows (How Everything Connects)

Flow 1 — Purchasing & Restocking Cycle
Supplier + Product → Purchase / Open PO (checked against Available Purchasing Budget; optional Owner Side Money Top-Up) → If Received: adds to Inventory Stock & updates Unit Cost; If Ordered: reserves Open PO Commitment → Posts paidAmountRWF outflow to Cash / MoMo / Bank → Posts remainingAmountRWF to Supplier Payables (Money I Owe) → Feeds RRA Input VAT.
Flow 2 — Sales, Profit & Customer Credit Cycle
Product (Cost + Price) + Customer → Sale → Automatically deducts Inventory Stock → Calculates COGS & Gross Profit → Posts paidAmountRWF inflow to Cash / MoMo / Bank → Posts remainingAmountRWF to Customer Receivables (Owed to Me) → Updates Cash Flow Curve, Branch Activity Heatmap, Sales Velocity, and RRA Output VAT / Turnover Tax.
Flow 3 — Expenses, Reserves & Executive Financial Clarity
Paid Expenses → Deduct from Cash / MoMo / Bank and reduce Net Profit → Establish the 15% Operational Safety Reserve (+ Unpaid Scheduled Bills) → Combined with Cash Channels and Open POs inside Where Is My Money? to compute your exact Available Purchasing Budget in real time.
Flow 4 — Multi-Branch Consolidation & Platform Governance
Every record carries ownerScopeKey + branchId → Sub-branch staff see only their assigned branchId under HQ permissions → Main HQ rolls up all branches into Branch Activity & Best Performer → Super Admin governs plan, paymentMessageCopy/paymentScreenshot, subscriptionExpiry, and live accountStatus enforcement.
